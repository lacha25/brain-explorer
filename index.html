ABOUT THIS WORKSPACE

Anatomy is the starting point.

Neuro Atlas connects named brain regions to their functions, learning mechanisms, and open questions. Functional roles overlap across circuits; each description is a starting point for reading the evidence.

What the geometry shows

The cortical surfaces come from the fsaverage5 reference brain. Atlas boundaries define anatomical parcels, not exclusive functional modules. Deep structures use labeled schematic markers unless an anatomical mesh is available.

How to read the evidence

“Supported” identifies a finding or mechanism with substantial relevant evidence. “Hypothesis” identifies a proposed account. “Mixed evidence” signals important qualifications. These labels are curated summaries, not systematic evidence grades.

Connections are conceptual

Lines show selected functional relationships. They are not reconstructed fiber tracts, and do not encode direction, strength, or exhaustive connectivity.
